About

Bridging learning and practice

I am an educational leader with over 10 years of experience in experiential learning, professional development, and career education across post-secondary and community settings. I love building impactful partnerships and developing innovative programs that bridge learning and practice, and I am committed to equitable, accessible, and transformative learning opportunities.

My work supports and mobilizes emerging leaders to pursue meaningful careers and make impactful contributions in their communities and institutions. Today, I lead graduate professional development at Western University, helping doctoral and graduate students recognize the value of their skills for careers within and beyond academia.

Selected work

2024 – Present · Western University

Own Your Future

I develop and coordinate Western's professional development program for graduate and doctoral students. The program helps students recognize and articulate the value of their graduate training for careers within and beyond academia, across five pillars: career engagement, communication and relationship building, leadership, teaching and learning, and thriving.

Recent work includes partnering with the Office of Indigenous Initiatives on two digital badge pathways in Indigenous allyship, and with Strengths at Western on a CliftonStrengths workshop series.

2021 – 2024 · King's University College

King's Promise & King's Job Shadow

I developed and coordinated career readiness programs for undergraduate students. King's Job Shadow paired students with partner organizations for one to two days of shadowing in roles that interested them, giving them a firsthand look at everyday tasks and expectations.

I built relationships with internal and external partners to offer experiential and work-integrated learning, and facilitated workshops, information sessions, and networking events.

2025 · Western University

Mobilizing Doctoral Students for Impactful Careers Beyond Academia

My doctoral dissertation-in-practice examines how to increase doctoral student engagement with non-academic career development. It frames career development as a sustainable development concern, connecting students' skills to the UN Sustainable Development Goals, and introduces the CARES framework to guide organizational change.

2024 · Canadian Journal for New Scholars in Education

Exploring Trends in BIPOC Student Engagement: A Review of the Literature

A co-authored literature review investigating why student engagement differs between BIPOC students and their White peers in Canadian higher education. The findings point to the importance of faculty representation, on-campus work opportunities, and alternative transit options.
